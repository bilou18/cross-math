Cross Math

A crossword built from arithmetic. Fill every blank so each row and column adds up — literally.

How it works

Every row and column is its own equation. Drag numbers from the tray below into the blanks so every line across and down solves correctly, all at once. On lines with several operations, × and ÷ are always calculated before + and −.
